Pairwise outcomes define a network of comparisons. The inferred ordering therefore depends not only on who wins, but on who encounters whom, how much evidence is available, and how competitors are grouped.

## Approach

I develop hierarchical Bradley–Terry-type models that infer latent skill and uncertainty from sparse or uneven comparison data. Group-level distributions provide a principled measure of the depth of competition while allowing information to be shared across related competitors.

## Current directions

The present work focuses on efficient inference, uncertainty calibration, ties, and comparisons between groups whose schedules and sample sizes differ.
