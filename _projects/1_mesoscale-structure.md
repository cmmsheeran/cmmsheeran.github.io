Complex systems are rarely observed at the level of every component. Instead, a readout turns microscopic variables into collective modes, modules, partitions, or order parameters. This project asks how the choice of readout interacts with network structure and dynamics to determine which mesoscopic patterns are visible, variable, and stable.

## Approach

I combine spectral representations of networks with stochastic dynamical models and probabilistic readouts. This makes it possible to derive distributions and moments of mesoscale observables, to distinguish robust structure from readout-dependent structure, and to identify the modes that control changes in representation.

## Current directions

Current applications include spontaneous neural cofluctuations, structure–function relationships in the brain, representational stability and drift, and the limits of spectral community inference.
